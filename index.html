Privacy Policy

Gujarati Vyakaran Guru - Mobile Application (v2.0)

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gujarati Vyakaran Guru ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, protects, and handles your information when you use our mobile application designed for Gujarati grammar and competitive exam preparation (GPSC, CCE, TET, TAT, Talati, Clerk). We are committed to safeguarding your personal privacy.
1
Information We Collect

When you register (Sign Up) or interact with the application, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Full Name: To identify your student profile, track progress, and customize certificates or learning results.
  • Date of Birth: Selected via the built-in calendar view to verify candidate eligibility for specific competitive exam age brackets.
  • Mobile Number (+91): Used as your unique account credential, authentication identifier, and for password recovery via SMS.
  • Account Password: Securely stored using cryptographic SHA-256 hash formatting to prevent unauthorized account access.
2
SMS Verification & Fast2SMS Gateway Policy

To ensure genuine account creation and prevent spam/automated bot registrations, we integrate with a certified telecom SMS gateway (Fast2SMS):

  • Daily OTP Limit: For security and spam prevention, a maximum of 1 OTP is permitted per mobile number per 24 hours.
  • 6-Digit Verification Code: The 6-digit OTP delivered via SMS remains valid for 15 minutes.
  • Zero Telemarketing / No Data Selling: We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade your phone number with any third-party marketing or commercial agencies.
3
Google AdMob Advertising Policy

To keep core study resources free for students, the app serves advertisements powered by Google AdMob:

  • Integrated Ad Formats: The application incorporates Banner Ads, Native Advanced Ads, and App Open Ad units.
  • Non-Personalized Device Identifiers: Google AdMob may utilize standard mobile advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID) and anonymized device telemetry to deliver and measure ads in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.
  • Ad-Free Experience for VIP Members: Users with an active VIP Premium subscription enjoy a completely ad-free learning experience across all app screens.
4
Payments & UPI Transaction Security

Subscriptions and VIP upgrades are processed through standard Indian UPI protocols:

  • Direct UPI App Flow: Payments are executed directly via installed, RBI-authorized UPI apps on your device (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).
  • No Sensitive Financial Storage: We never collect, process, or store your bank account numbers, debit/credit card details, CVVs, or UPI PINs within our application.
  • UTR / Transaction Verification: After completing the payment, users provide the 12-digit UPI reference number (UTR) for administrative verification before premium course features are activated.
5
Data Protection & Administrative Controls

We employ industry-standard safeguards to protect your registered credentials:

  • Encrypted Admin Access: The Administrative Panel is protected by cryptographic SHA-256 password authentication.
  • Restricted Access: Administrative audit tools are strictly used for user verification, password recovery support, and VIP premium status toggling.
  • Account Deletion: Users may request account deletion or data erasure at any time by contacting our support team via email or WhatsApp.
6
Children's Privacy & Educational Use

Our application is intended for students and candidates preparing for government and educational competitive examinations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without appropriate parental or guardian consent.
